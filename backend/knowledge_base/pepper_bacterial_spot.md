# Pepper Bell - Bacterial Spot

## Crop
Bell Pepper

## Disease
Bacterial Spot

## Cause
Bacteria of the genus Xanthomonas.

## Symptoms
- Small water-soaked or dark spots can appear on leaves.
- Lesions may enlarge and become brown or necrotic.
- Severe infections can cause leaf drop.
- Fruit may develop raised, scab-like spots.

## Favorable Conditions
- Warm and humid conditions favor disease development.
- Splashing water can spread bacteria between plants.

## Prevention
- Use healthy or certified disease-free planting material.
- Avoid working with plants when foliage is wet.
- Improve air circulation.
- Remove severely infected plant material.
- Avoid overhead irrigation where practical.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove severely affected leaves and plant debris.
- Maintain adequate spacing between plants.
- Sanitize tools used on infected plants.

### Chemical
- Use only locally registered products labeled for bacterial diseases of pepper.
- Follow the product label and local agricultural recommendations.

## Important Note
Symptoms can resemble other bacterial or fungal diseases. Image-based classification should be treated as an initial diagnosis rather than definitive laboratory confirmation.
