# Potato - Late Blight

## Crop
Potato

## Disease
Late Blight

## Cause
Primarily caused by the oomycete Phytophthora infestans.

## Symptoms
- Irregular dark green to brown lesions may develop on leaves.
- Lesions can expand rapidly under favorable conditions.
- White fungal-like growth may appear around lesions under humid conditions.
- Tubers can develop brown or reddish-brown internal discoloration.

## Favorable Conditions
- Cool to moderate temperatures combined with high humidity or prolonged leaf wetness favor disease development.
- Wind-driven rain and water can spread spores between plants.

## Prevention
- Use healthy planting material.
- Monitor plants regularly during favorable weather.
- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness where practical.
- Remove infected plant material appropriately.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove severely infected plant material.
- Destroy volunteer potato plants and crop debris where appropriate.
- Avoid planting infected tubers.

### Chemical
- Fungicides may be required for management under high-risk conditions.
- Use only locally registered products and follow their labels and local recommendations.

## Important Note
Late blight can spread rapidly. Early detection and timely management are important.
