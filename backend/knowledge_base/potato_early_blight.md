# Potato - Early Blight

## Crop
Potato

## Disease
Early Blight

## Cause
Primarily caused by the fungus Alternaria solani.

## Symptoms
- Small dark brown to black lesions can appear on older leaves.
- Lesions may develop characteristic concentric rings.
- Affected leaves may yellow and eventually die.
- Disease can reduce photosynthetic leaf area.

## Favorable Conditions
- Warm conditions can favor disease development.
- Alternating periods of wetness and dryness can contribute to disease development.
- Plant stress can increase susceptibility.

## Prevention
- Maintain good plant vigor.
- Remove infected plant debris after harvest.
- Practice crop rotation where practical.
- Avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove or destroy infected plant debris.
- Maintain adequate plant nutrition and irrigation.
- Use crop rotation where practical.

### Chemical
- Fungicides may be used when appropriate and locally registered.
- Follow the product label and local agricultural recommendations.

## Important Note
Early blight can be confused with other leaf-spot diseases. Confirm diagnosis using additional plant pathology information when necessary.
