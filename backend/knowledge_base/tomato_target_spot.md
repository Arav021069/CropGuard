# Tomato - Target Spot

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Target Spot

## Cause
The fungus Corynespora cassiicola.

## Symptoms
- Circular brown lesions may appear on leaves.
- Lesions can develop concentric rings resembling a target.
- Severe infections can cause leaf drop.
- Fruit may develop dark, sunken lesions.

## Favorable Conditions
- Warm and humid conditions.
- Extended leaf wetness.
- Dense foliage and poor air circulation.

## Prevention
- Improve air circulation.
- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
- Remove infected plant debris.
- Maintain appropriate plant spacing.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove severely infected leaves.
- Remove crop debris after harvest.
- Maintain good plant vigor.

### Chemical
- Fungicides may be used when appropriate and locally registered.
- Follow product labels and local recommendations.
