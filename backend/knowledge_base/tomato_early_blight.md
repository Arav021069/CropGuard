# Tomato - Early Blight

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Early Blight

## Cause
Primarily associated with Alternaria species, especially Alternaria solani.

## Symptoms
- Brown to dark lesions may develop on older leaves.
- Lesions may show concentric ring patterns.
- Surrounding tissue may turn yellow.
- Severe infections can cause premature leaf loss.
- Fruit near the stem may develop dark, sunken lesions.

## Favorable Conditions
- Warm conditions and prolonged periods of leaf wetness favor disease development.
- Plant stress can increase susceptibility.

## Prevention
- Remove infected plant debris.
- Maintain good plant spacing and air circulation.
- Avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.
- Practice crop rotation where practical.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove severely affected leaves.
- Remove crop debris after harvest.
- Maintain adequate plant vigor.

### Chemical
- Fungicides may be used where appropriate and locally registered.
- Follow product labels and local agricultural recommendations.
