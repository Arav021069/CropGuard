# Tomato - Late Blight

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Late Blight

## Cause
Primarily caused by Phytophthora infestans.

## Symptoms
- Irregular water-soaked green to brown lesions can develop on leaves.
- Lesions may expand rapidly.
- Under humid conditions, pale fungal-like growth may occur around lesions.
- Stems and fruit can also become affected.

## Favorable Conditions
- Cool to moderate temperatures.
- High humidity.
- Extended periods of leaf wetness.
- Rain and wind can assist disease spread.

## Prevention
- Monitor plants regularly during favorable weather.
- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
- Remove infected plant material.
- Maintain good air circulation.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove severely infected plant material.
- Avoid handling healthy plants after handling infected material without sanitation.
- Remove crop debris appropriately.

### Chemical
- Fungicides may be necessary under disease-favorable conditions.
- Use only locally registered products and follow label directions.

## Important Note
Late blight can develop and spread rapidly, so early detection is important.
