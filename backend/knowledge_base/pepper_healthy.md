# Pepper Bell - Healthy

## Crop
Bell Pepper

## Disease
Healthy

## Symptoms
- Leaves appear generally green and structurally normal.
- No obvious characteristic disease lesions are present.

## Management
- Maintain adequate irrigation without prolonged leaf wetness.
- Provide appropriate nutrition.
- Monitor plants regularly for changes in leaf color, spots, wilting, or pest damage.
- Remove diseased plant material promptly if symptoms appear.

## Important Note
A healthy classification means that the model did not detect the visual characteristics associated with the diseases in its training classes. It does not guarantee that the plant is completely free from pests, nutrient deficiencies, or diseases outside the model's classes.
