# Tomato - Bacterial Spot

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Bacterial Spot

## Cause
Bacteria belonging to the genus Xanthomonas.

## Symptoms
- Small dark or water-soaked spots can appear on leaves.
- Lesions may become brown and necrotic.
- Severe infections can cause leaf drop.
- Fruit may develop small raised or scabby lesions.

## Favorable Conditions
- Warm and humid conditions favor disease development.
- Splashing water can spread the bacteria.

## Prevention
- Use disease-free seed or transplants.
- Avoid working with plants while foliage is wet.
- Improve air circulation.
- Remove infected plant debris.
- Avoid overhead irrigation where practical.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove severely infected plant material.
- Sanitize tools.
- Maintain appropriate plant spacing.

### Chemical
- Use only locally registered products labeled for bacterial diseases of tomato.
- Follow product labels and local recommendations.

## Important Note
Visual symptoms can overlap with other diseases. Use additional diagnostic information when necessary.
