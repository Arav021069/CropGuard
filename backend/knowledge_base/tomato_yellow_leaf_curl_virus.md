# Tomato - Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus

## Cause
A group of begomoviruses, commonly transmitted by whiteflies.

## Symptoms
- Young leaves may curl upward.
- Leaves can become yellow.
- Plants may become stunted.
- Fruit production can be reduced.
- Symptoms can vary depending on virus strain and plant conditions.

## Transmission
- Primarily transmitted by whiteflies.
- Infected plant material can contribute to disease spread.

## Prevention
- Use healthy planting material.
- Monitor and manage whitefly populations.
- Remove severely infected plants where appropriate.
- Control weeds that may serve as hosts.
- Use appropriate physical or cultural barriers where practical.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove infected plants according to local recommendations.
- Manage whitefly populations.
- Remove alternative host weeds.
- Maintain good crop hygiene.

### Chemical
- Use only locally registered products for the relevant pest and crop.
- Follow product labels and integrated pest management recommendations.

## Important Note
The virus itself cannot generally be controlled after infection; management focuses heavily on prevention and vector control.
