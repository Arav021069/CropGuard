# Tomato - Leaf Mold

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Leaf Mold

## Cause
Commonly associated with the fungus Passalora fulva.

## Symptoms
- Pale green to yellow spots can appear on the upper surface of leaves.
- Olive-green to brown velvety growth may appear on the underside.
- Severe infections can cause leaves to curl and die.
- Disease is more common in protected or humid environments.

## Favorable Conditions
- High humidity strongly favors development.
- Poor air circulation increases risk.

## Prevention
- Improve ventilation and air circulation.
- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
- Maintain appropriate plant spacing.
- Remove infected leaves and debris.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove affected leaves.
- Reduce humidity where possible.
- Improve greenhouse ventilation where applicable.

### Chemical
- Appropriate fungicides may be used where locally registered.
- Follow product labels and local recommendations.
