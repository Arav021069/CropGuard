# Tomato - Septoria Leaf Spot

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Septoria Leaf Spot

## Cause
The fungus Septoria lycopersici.

## Symptoms
- Numerous small circular spots develop on leaves.
- Lesions often have dark margins and lighter centers.
- Small dark structures may be visible within lesions.
- Severe infection can cause extensive leaf loss.

## Favorable Conditions
- Warm and humid conditions.
- Extended periods of leaf wetness.
- Splashing water can spread spores.

## Prevention
- Remove infected plant debris.
- Avoid overhead irrigation where practical.
- Improve air circulation.
- Maintain adequate plant spacing.
- Practice crop rotation where practical.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove affected leaves.
- Remove crop debris after harvest.
- Sanitize tools used around infected plants.

### Chemical
- Fungicides may be used where appropriate and locally registered.
- Follow product labels and local recommendations.
