# Tomato - Tomato Mosaic Virus

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Tomato Mosaic Virus

## Cause
Tomato mosaic virus (ToMV).

## Symptoms
- Leaves may show mottled light and dark green patterns.
- Leaves may become distorted.
- Plants can show reduced growth.
- Fruit development and quality may be affected.

## Transmission
- The virus can spread mechanically through contaminated hands, tools, clothing, and plant contact.
- Infected plant material can be an important source of infection.

## Prevention
- Use healthy seed and planting material.
- Sanitize tools and hands.
- Avoid handling healthy plants immediately after infected plants.
- Remove infected plants where appropriate.
- Control weeds and volunteer plants that may harbor the virus.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove infected plants according to local recommendations.
- Clean and disinfect tools.
- Avoid unnecessary plant-to-plant contact.
- Use resistant varieties when available and appropriate.

### Chemical
- There is no direct chemical cure that eliminates the virus from an infected plant.
- Chemical management should focus only on relevant vectors or secondary problems where applicable.

## Important Note
Management focuses primarily on preventing introduction and spread.
