# Tomato - Spider Mites

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Two-Spotted Spider Mite Damage

## Cause
Feeding damage caused by spider mites, particularly Tetranychus urticae and related species.

## Symptoms
- Fine pale or yellow stippling may appear on leaves.
- Leaves may become bronze or yellow under severe infestation.
- Fine webbing can appear on heavily infested plants.
- Severe infestations can cause leaf drying and plant stress.

## Favorable Conditions
- Hot and dry conditions can favor spider mite population growth.
- Dusty conditions can contribute to mite problems.

## Prevention
- Monitor the undersides of leaves regularly.
- Maintain adequate plant health and irrigation.
- Avoid unnecessary disruption of beneficial predatory mites and insects.

## Management
### Cultural
- Remove heavily infested plant material where appropriate.
- Use strong water sprays where suitable to dislodge mites.
- Encourage beneficial predatory organisms.

### Chemical
- Use only products specifically registered for mite management on tomato.
- Follow product labels and local agricultural recommendations.
- Avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum pesticide use because it can harm natural enemies.

## Important Note
Spider mite damage is caused by a pest rather than a plant pathogen.
